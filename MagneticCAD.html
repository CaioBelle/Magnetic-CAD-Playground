Permanent magnet
Outer radius = inner radius + thickness
The object origin is the centre of curvature. Base radius is the inner spherical surface; thickness grows radially outward. Horizontal/vertical angular spans are symmetric around local +Z.
Resize works on magnets: cuboids bake X/Y/Z scaling into their dimensions; cylinders bake X/Y into radius and Z into height; spherical segments preserve their spherical curvature by baking the mean XYZ scale into both radii.
Current coil
Infinite-line representation: the CAD uses a long visual segment along local X. Its finite Lorentz-integration length is chosen per line in the Radia solve menu.
The solenoid is represented as an explicit helix around local Z. CAD/Figure Studio automatically limit only the displayed wire thickness when neighbouring turns would visually overlap; Radia still uses the entered wire radius.

Circular pattern about a global axis
Original + 4 copies · angular step 72°.
The original remains in place. Copies are equally spaced using: total angle ÷ (copies + 1). For 4 copies over 360°, the step is 72°.
Spherical lattice around the selected shell magnet
Centered 5 × 3 spherical lattice.
All copies share the same sphere centre as the selected magnet. Their local +Z axes move over the sphere according to the horizontal/vertical angular pitch. Use Revolve about axis with the same pivot to create a complete latitude ring.
Polar spherical coverage
The selected circular magnet is the top polar magnet. Its diameter and thickness are never changed.
Automatic ring proposal.
The selected magnet is the fixed top pole. Alternating topology requires an even number of intermediate rings and an even magnet count on every intermediate ring. “Optimize positions” preserves those counts; “Auto layout” regenerates an even-parity topology.
Linear pattern
Original + 3 copies along the construction line.
Each new body is displaced by another multiple of this step vector, while preserving its orientation and source properties.
Mirror about a global plane
Previewing the mirrored body and construction plane.
Offset is measured along the plane normal: Z for XY, X for YZ, and Y for XZ. Magnetization and coil-current normals are mirrored consistently.

Source discretization
Permanent magnets are exported with the closest reliable Radia primitive available: native rectangular PMs for cuboids, native polygonal cylindrical PMs for disks, and convex tessellated shell pieces for spherical segments. The generated result stores a PM fidelity report.
The optional check rebuilds only discretized PM shapes at 2× geometric resolution and compares B on a sparse 4×4×4 probe grid. It is intended as a convergence warning, not a replacement for a final mesh/refinement study.
The script produces one portable .mfield result file.
