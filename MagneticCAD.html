Circular pattern about a global axis
Original + 4 copies · angular step 72°.
The original remains in place. Copies are equally spaced using: total angle ÷ (copies + 1). For 4 copies over 360°, the step is 72°.
Linear pattern
Original + 3 copies along the construction line.
Each new body is displaced by another multiple of this step vector, while preserving its orientation and source properties.
